Smart Kids World - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Smart Kids World is an educational game for children. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping the app simple and safe.

Information We Collect

Smart Kids World does not intentionally collect, request, or store personal information such as names, addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or contact information.

Children's Privacy

The game is designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Local Game Data

The game may save game progress, such as stars or completed activities, locally on the device. This information is used only to provide the game experience and is not intentionally sent to us.

Third-Party Services

Smart Kids World does not intentionally share personal information with third parties.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when needed. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the email address associated with the Smart Kids World developer account.
